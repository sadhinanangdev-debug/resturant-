Our Story

Since 2010

Spice Garden started as a small family kitchen se start kiya tha. Hamara maqsad tha logon ko ghar jaisa asli khana khilana. Aaj 14 saal baad, hum sukkur ke sab se mashoor restaurants me se ek hain.

Hum sirf fresh local sabzi, asli masalay, aur pure ghee use karte hain. Koi artificial color ya preservative nahi. Har dish hamare head chef khud check karte hain.

20+ years experience in 5-star hotels.lahore, hyderabad, sukkur me kaam kiya.
